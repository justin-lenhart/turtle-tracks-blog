I'm Justin — a son, brother, boyfriend, airline pilot, traveler, and big time nerd. Most of my week is
spent somewhere other than home.

This site is where I keep the things I'd otherwise lose: short reports from
trips, build logs for whatever's on the bench or in the shop, and a map of
everywhere I've flown.

It's mostly for me. If you found it some other way, the
[projects](/projects/) are probably the interesting part. Sometimes I go to interesting places also.
